Know Our Story

Who We Are

Sonali Homes LLP is a leading real estate developer in Kalyan East, committed to creating high-quality residential spaces that inspire modern living. With over 15 years of experience in the construction industry, we have established ourselves as a trusted name in real estate development.

Our focus on excellence, transparency, and customer satisfaction has helped us deliver dream homes to more than 500 happy families. We believe in providing homes that stand the test of time, combining quality construction with innovative design.

At Sonali Homes, we don't just build structures; we create communities where families can thrive and create lasting memories. Every project is a testament to our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

To create homes that combine quality, innovation, and sustainability — making dream homes accessible to every family. We envision a world where every family has access to their dream home that is not only beautiful but also sustainable and affordable.

Mission

To continuously improve construction excellence and deliver projects on time with unmatched quality and trust. We strive to maintain lifelong relationships with our customers by providing exceptional service and support.
